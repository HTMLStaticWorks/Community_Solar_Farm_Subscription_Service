Who We Are

Clean Energy Access For All Neighborhoods

Over 75% of American households cannot install rooftop solar panels due to renting, shaded roofs, HOA rules, or upfront financial barriers. Sunova was established to eliminate these barriers completely.

We build and operate local, ground-mounted community solar arrays. When local residents subscribe, the clean energy generated feeds directly into the regional utility grid, and subscribers receive monthly credit allocations that reduce their power bills by 10% to 15% guaranteed.

Our Mission

To democratize solar energy by offering flexible, $0-upfront subscriptions to neighborhood solar farms, guaranteeing lower monthly electricity costs while accelerating grid decarbonization.

Our Vision

A future where every home, apartment, and small business can seamlessly power their daily life with local renewable solar credits without ever needing rooftop hardware.
