The Billing Cycle

How Utility Bill Credits Work

1. Solar Farm Generates Credits

Your allocated portion of the local community solar array generates clean electricity that flows into the regional grid.

2. Utility Credits Your Statement

Your electric utility credits the value of those solar kilowatt-hours directly on your monthly power bill.

3. You Keep The 10%–15% Difference

You pay Sunova for the solar credits at a guaranteed 10%–15% discount, pocketing pure monthly savings.
