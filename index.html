How Community Solar Works

4 Steps To Monthly Solar Credits

1. Reserve Your Share

Sign up online in 2 minutes. We match a farm share to your monthly kilowatt usage.

2. Farm Generates Power

Our local solar farm generates clean energy and feeds electricity straight into the grid.

3. Earn Solar Credits

Your utility awards energy credits directly to your account based on your share's output.

4. Guaranteed Savings

You pay a discounted subscription rate for credits and save 10% to 15% net every month.

Why Community Solar?

The Smarter, Friction-Free Way to Go Solar

Traditional solar requires large upfront loans, roof inspections, and long installations. With Sunova, you get all the environmental and financial benefits without touching your roof.

  • Zero Upfront Cost: Never pay for panels, permits, or maintenance.
  • Renters & Condos Welcome: Available to anyone who pays an electric bill.
  • Seamless Utility Integration: Credits apply straight to your current bill.
  • Real-Time Subscriber Dashboard: Track credit allocations and CO2 savings 24/7.
